Privacy Policy

Effective from 24 August 2026 · Last updated 24 August 2026

The short version

  • Bookends doesn't have accounts, passwords, or email sign-up.
  • If you save a score, we store the name you type and your score and guessed words for that day.
  • That leaderboard is visible to anyone who opens the game link – it isn't private.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics, or advertising of our own.
  • Our hosting provider (Netlify) may collect standard technical logs, as any website host does.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers Bookends (referred to here as "we", "us", or "the game"), a word puzzle available at this website. It's currently run as a personal / hobby project rather than a registered company.

Information we collect

We collect very little, and only what you choose to give us:

  • A display name. When you save your score, you type a name of your choosing. We don't verify this is your real name, and there's no way for us to link it to your real identity.
  • Your game results. Your daily score, and the words you submitted for the Longest and Shortest word categories, are saved alongside your chosen name.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment details, or any other personal information, because the game doesn't currently ask for any.

Automatically collected information

Like virtually every website, our hosting provider (Netlify) may automatically log standard technical data when you visit – such as IP address, browser type, and the time of your visit. This happens at the hosting infrastructure level; we don't actively access, analyse, or use this data ourselves. You can read Netlify's own privacy policy for details on what they collect: netlify.com/privacy.

We don't set any cookies of our own. Netlify's platform may use a small number of strictly necessary technical cookies to operate the service.

How your information is stored

Game data (names, scores, and answers) is stored using Netlify's data storage service ("Netlify Blobs"). Specifically:

  • Your daily leaderboard entry (name and score) is stored as shared data – meaning anyone who has the link to this game can see it, not just you. Please don't use your real full name or anything sensitive as your display name.
  • Your in-progress puzzle answers for the current day are stored privately to your own device/browser, and aren't shared with other players.

Why we process this information

We only use your information to run the core feature of the game: showing a daily leaderboard so you and whoever you share the game with can compare scores. We don't use it for advertising, marketing, or profiling, and we don't sell or share it with any third party beyond our hosting provider.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard data is currently kept indefinitely, as the game has no automatic deletion process in place yet. If you'd like your name and score removed from a leaderboard, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it manually.

Sharing with third parties

We don't sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers. The only third party involved is Netlify, our hosting and data storage provider, which processes data on our behalf in order to run the website.

Where your information is stored

Data is stored and processed on Netlify's infrastructure. Netlify may process data in various locations depending on their own infrastructure setup – see their privacy policy for current details on data locations and safeguards.

Children's privacy

Bookends is a general-audience word game and is not specifically directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information (such as an identifying name), please contact us and we'll remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold that relates to you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Object to how it's used

Because we only hold a self-chosen display name and a game score, exercising these rights is simple – just contact us with the name you used, and we'll remove the associated entry.

If you're in the UK and unhappy with how we've handled your information, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

Changes to this policy

If Bookends adds new features in future – such as user accounts, sign-in, or payments – this policy will be updated to reflect that, and the "last updated" date at the top will change. We'd encourage you to check back occasionally if you're a regular player.
