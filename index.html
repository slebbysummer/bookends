❓ How to play

Every word you enter must start with today's first letter and end with today's last letter.

  • 📏 Longest word – score = its length. Longer wins bigger.
  • ✂️ Shortest word – score rewards brevity. Shorter wins bigger.
  • 🎉 3 bonus categories (random each puzzle) – 1 point per letter if your word fits.
  • 🏳️ Stuck? Hit "Give up" on any category to see example answers – locks it in at 0 points.
  • 🏆 Total score is the sum of everything you've locked in – save it to today's leaderboard when you're done.
