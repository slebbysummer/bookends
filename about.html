A quick note about Bookends

This is a hobby project, made by one person, still being actively worked on.

That means it's genuinely still a work in progress. We're actively reviewing, expanding and correcting the answer lists behind each bonus category to make sure they're as accurate and fair as possible – but with tens of thousands of words across dozens of categories, some gaps, oddities or the occasional wrong answer are still likely.

📲 Tip: you can now add Bookends to your phone's home screen for quick access, just like a regular app. On iPhone, tap the Share button in your browser and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, look for an install prompt in your browser menu.

What we're working on

  • Replacing hand-typed lists with properly sourced, verified data where we can
  • Trimming categories that lean too obscure, so answers feel findable rather than trivia-only
  • Fixing genuine bugs as they're spotted
  • Adding new categories people suggest

Frequently asked questions

What is Bookends?

Bookends is a free daily word game. Every day you get a starting letter and an ending letter, and the challenge is to find words that begin and end with those two letters.

How do I play?

Enter words that start with today's first letter and finish with today's last letter. Longer words score more in the Longest word category, shorter words score more in the Shortest word category, and there are three bonus categories each day too – only your best bonus category counts toward your total.

Is Bookends free?

Yes. Bookends is completely free to play, with no account, sign-up, or download required.

Is there a new puzzle every day?

Yes – a new puzzle goes live every day at midnight UK time.

Can I play Bookends on my phone?

Yes. Bookends works in any web browser on phones, tablets and computers.

How is my score calculated?

Your total is the sum of your Longest word, Shortest word, and your single best bonus category for the day. Longer words score more in Longest; shorter words score more in Shortest; bonus categories score one point per letter.

What happens if I give up on a category?

You'll be shown an example answer, but that category locks in at 0 points for the day.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Just type a name if you want to save your score to that day's leaderboard – there's no password or email required.

Found something? Tell us.

If a word didn't work that should have, an answer feels wrong, something broke or you've got an idea for a new category – we'd genuinely like to hear it. This game will get better as people tell us what's not quite right yet.
