A quick note about Bookends

This is a hobby project, made by one person, still being actively worked on.

That means it's genuinely still a work in progress. We're actively reviewing, expanding and correcting the answer lists behind each bonus category to make sure they're as accurate and fair as possible – but with tens of thousands of words across dozens of categories, some gaps, oddities or the occasional wrong answer are still likely.

What we're working on

  • Replacing hand-typed lists with properly sourced, verified data where we can
  • Trimming categories that lean too obscure, so answers feel findable rather than trivia-only
  • Fixing genuine bugs as they're spotted
  • Adding new categories people suggest

Found something? Tell us.

If a word didn't work that should have, an answer feels wrong, something broke or you've got an idea for a new category – we'd genuinely like to hear it. This game will get better as people tell us what's not quite right yet.

Email bookendsgame@gmail.com

Thanks for playing and for bearing with the rough edges while we sand them down.
